# Panda Express: catering menu and policies (OpenBid demo snapshot, 2026-11-20)

Source: https://www.pandaexpress.com/catering (hand-built snapshot for the OpenBid demo; not live data).

## Catering menu (individual plates)
- Catering Plate: Orange Chicken + Chow Mein: $10.50 per plate
- Catering Plate: Broccoli Beef + Fried Rice: $10.90 per plate
- Catering Plate: Kung Pao Chicken + White Rice: $10.50 per plate. Contains peanuts.
- Vegetarian Plate: Eggplant Tofu + Super Greens + Chow Mein: $9.90 per plate. Vegetarian.

## Dietary information
Panda Express does not label any menu item as vegan. Eggplant Tofu and Super Greens are vegetarian, but sauces and shared cooking equipment mean we cannot confirm any plate is vegan. Chow Mein contains egg-free noodles but is cooked in shared woks.

## Allergen policy
Peanuts are an ingredient in Kung Pao Chicken and are present in our kitchens. All entrées are prepared in shared woks and on shared surfaces. We cannot guarantee that any menu item is free of peanuts or other allergens.

## Delivery and catering policy
- Catering delivery fee: $9.99 per order.
- Catering minimum order: $150 before tax.
- Catering orders require at least 60 minutes of lead time.
- Delivery arrives within a window ending 10 minutes before the requested time.
- Maximum 100 plates per catering order.

## Hours
Daily 10:00 AM to 9:30 PM.
