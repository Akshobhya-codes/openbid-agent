# Chipotle: catering menu and policies (OpenBid demo snapshot, 2026-11-20)

Source: https://www.chipotle.com/catering (hand-built snapshot for the OpenBid demo; not live data).

## Catering menu (individually packaged bowls)
- Individual Chicken Burrito Bowl: $12.45 each
- Individual Steak Burrito Bowl: $13.95 each
- Individual Veggie Bowl with Guacamole: $11.45 each. Vegetarian.
- Individual Sofritas Bowl: $11.45 each. Vegan when ordered without cheese or sour cream (the default for catering sofritas bowls).

## Dietary information
Sofritas, white and brown rice, black and pinto beans, fajita vegetables, salsas, and guacamole are vegan. Veggie bowls include cheese and sour cream and are vegetarian, not vegan.

## Allergen policy
None of Chipotle's ingredients contain peanuts or tree nuts. Food is prepared in shared kitchens where cross-contact with other allergens is possible, so we cannot guarantee any item is allergen-free.

## Delivery and catering policy
- Catering delivery fee: $4.99 per order.
- Catering minimum order: $100 before tax.
- Catering orders require at least 45 minutes of lead time.
- Delivery arrives within a window ending 20 minutes before the requested time.

## Hours
Daily 10:45 AM to 10:00 PM.
